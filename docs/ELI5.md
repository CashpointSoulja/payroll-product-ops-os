# ELI5: what is this?

Imagine a big company that pays people in lots of countries. Every country has its own rules: how much you get when you're sick, when the tax year ends, which forms go to the government and by when.

Lots of people tell the company when something goes wrong: the people being paid, the companies paying them, the support team, the sales team, the payroll experts in each country. They all shout at once, in different places.

This page is the **notice board, the to-do list and the diary** for the person whose job is to make sense of all that shouting.

1. **Intake (the notice board).** Every complaint gets pinned up. Complaints about the same thing get pinned together, so you can see "35 people said this", not 35 separate notes. Then each one gets a score out of 100: how many customers it hurts, how much money is at stake, whether it breaks the law, and whether it fits what we're building anyway. Anything that breaks the law always goes to the top. No arguing.

2. **GTM rollout (the launch checklist).** When we build a fix, we can't switch it on everywhere at once. Each country needs its lawyers, its payroll partners, its support team, its money people and its sales team to be ready. The grid shows who is ready and who isn't, with a red flag on anything that could go wrong.

3. **Root cause (the detective's notebook).** When something breaks, we write down every guess about why, then check each guess against the facts. Wrong guesses get crossed out. The right one gets fixed. And we don't call it fixed until the numbers show the problem has actually gone away.

4. **Cadence (the weekly timetable).** Every week has the same rhythm: Monday sort the new complaints, Tuesday check upcoming law changes, Wednesday decide what to build, Thursday check launches and investigations, Friday write it all down for everyone. The agenda writes itself from the other three pages.

That's it: **hear everything, count it, rank it fairly, ship it carefully, prove the fix worked, and do it every week.**

*Everything on the page is made-up example data.*
